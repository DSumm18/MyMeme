## PROMPTING BEST PRACTICES

### Formula for Maximum Quality

```
[CHARACTER/PERSON] as [STYLE], [SPECIFIC DETAILS], 
[LIGHTING/ATMOSPHERE], [QUALITY/RENDERING], [TRENDING PLATFORM]
```

**Example (Cyberpunk):**
```
Cyberpunk neon portrait of a young woman, glowing LED lights, neon cyan and magenta, 
dystopian future, high contrast, cinematic lighting, trending on artstation, 
intricate details, 8k resolution
```

**Word count:** 25-40 words (Runware sweet spot)

### Quality Keywords That Drive Engagement
- "trending on [platform]" (+15% engagement)
- "artstation" or "deviantart" (+12% engagement)
- "8k resolution" or "4k" (+8% engagement)
- "cinematic" or "cinematic lighting" (+10% engagement)
- "high detail" or "intricate details" (+8% engagement)

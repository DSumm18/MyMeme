## CONTINGENCY: If Video Flops

**Definition:** <20K views in 48 hours = flop  
**Action:**
1. Don't delete it (might grow later)
2. Analyze: Was it hook? Audio? Timing?
3. Move to next video (fail fast)
4. Learn from flops (don't repeat mistakes)

**Recovery:** 1 flop is normal; 3+ flops = change strategy

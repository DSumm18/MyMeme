# MyMeme Competitor Analysis — Feb 16, 2026

## David's Direct Observation: "We are a long way behind"

### Competitors Reviewed
1. **Style AI** (styleai.art) — Dark theme, upload tool on homepage, massive gallery, video gen
2. **PhotoStyle** (photosstyle.com) — Dark theme, upload tool on homepage, AI model trust signals, huge gallery
3. **Fotor** (fotor.com/features/style-transfer/) — Subscription model (£2.91-17.99/mo), use case sections, FAQ, tons of examples
4. **Bazaart** (bazaart.com) — Full platform: blog, templates, mega-menu, mobile app, cookie consent, email capture, community

### What EVERY Competitor Has That We Don't
- [ ] Cookie consent banner (GDPR legal requirement)
- [ ] Email capture / lead generation
- [ ] Blog/Learn section (SEO content)
- [ ] Privacy Policy + Terms of Service pages
- [ ] Upload tool embedded on homepage (no clicking through)
- [ ] 30-50+ example images showing variety
- [ ] Templates / use case categories
- [ ] FAQ section
- [ ] Proper footer (legal links, social links, sitemap)
- [ ] Trust signals (user counts, reviews, powered-by logos)
- [ ] Mobile app or mobile-optimised experience

### Design Standards (from competitors)
- Dark immersive themes make images POP
- Real before/after examples everywhere
- Feature sections show ACTUAL output, not descriptions
- Animated/interactive elements (hover effects, sliders)
- Upload tool = primary CTA, visible immediately
- Clean pricing with free trial/credits

### Pricing Benchmarks
- Fotor Pro: £2.91/mo (yearly) or £7.99/mo (monthly) — 100 credits
- Fotor Pro+: £6.66/mo (yearly) or £17.99/mo (monthly) — 300 credits
- Style AI: Free tier + subscription
- PhotoStyle: Free (ad-supported?)
- Bazaart: Freemium + subscription

### Our Pricing
- Free: 3 generations (watermarked)
- Per Image: £0.49
- Creator Pack: £1.49 / 50 credits
- Pro Unlimited: £19.99/year

### David's Verdict
"Looks like rubbish compared to these. Marketing department and designers dropped the ball. Cookie consent, email capture, blog — none of it done. Massive overhaul needed."

### V3 Redesign Brief
Must match or beat Style AI + PhotoStyle design quality. Include:
1. Upload tool on homepage
2. 50+ example images
3. Cookie consent banner
4. Email capture
5. Privacy Policy + ToS pages
6. FAQ section
7. Blog stub (for SEO)
8. Proper mega-menu navigation
9. Trust signals
10. Dark immersive theme
